# R6Champs - Rainbow Six Siege Map Strategy Tool

Welcome to **R6Champs**, a tactical reference and strategy planning tool designed for Rainbow Six Siege players. Plan your attacks and defenses with interactive map blueprints, drawing tools, and operator placement.

## Features

- **16 Tactical Maps** - Bank, Border, Chalet, Clubhouse, Coastline, Consulate, Emerald Plains, Kafe Dostoyevsky, Kanal, Lair, Nighthaven Labs, Oregon, Outback, Skyscraper, Theme Park, and Villa
- **Multi-Floor Support** - Switch between basement, ground floor, and upper floors for each map
- **Drawing Tools** - Draw on maps with a pencil tool, adjustable brush sizes, and color selection
- **Strategy Management** - Create, save, and load multiple strategies per map
- **Operator Icons** - Place operator icons on maps to plan team positions
- **No Account Required** - All your strategies are saved locally in your browser
- **Dark Theme** - Easy on the eyes with a sleek dark interface

## Getting Started

1. **Browse Maps** - Visit the homepage to see all available maps
2. **Select a Map** - Click on any map to open its blueprint
3. **Choose a Floor** - Use the floor tabs to switch between levels
4. **Create a Strategy** - Click "New Strategy" in the sidebar to start planning
5. **Draw & Place Icons** - Use the drawing tools and operator icons to mark positions
6. **Save Your Work** - Click "Save Strategy" to store it locally

## How to Use

### Drawing on Maps
- **Pencil Tool** (P) - Draw tactics, callouts, and routes
- **Color Picker** - Choose from multiple colors for different purposes
- **Brush Size** - Adjust the brush thickness with the slider
- **Eraser** (E) - Remove individual strokes
- **Clear Canvas** - Erase all drawings to start fresh

### Managing Strategies
- **New Strategy** - Create a fresh strategy with a custom name
- **Save Strategy** - Store your current drawings and icon placements
- **Load Strategy** - Switch between saved strategies instantly
- **Edit Strategy** - Modify any saved strategy by clicking Edit
- **Delete Strategy** - Remove strategies you no longer need

### Operator Icons
- Click an operator icon to select it
- Click on the map to place it
- Drag icons to reposition them
- Hover and click the × to delete

## Browser Storage

All your strategies are saved using browser localStorage:
- **Storage Limit**: ~5MB per browser
- **Duration**: Persists indefinitely until you clear browser data
- **Privacy**: Everything stays on your device - no servers or accounts needed
- **Backup**: Consider taking screenshots of important strategies

## Troubleshooting

### Strategies Not Saving?
- Check that your browser allows localStorage
- Try clearing old strategies if you're out of space
- Check the browser console for errors (F12)

### Icons Not Showing?
- Refresh the page
- Check your internet connection for image loading
- Verify you're on the correct floor
